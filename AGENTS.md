## Project rules
- Site copy shared by pages and JSON-LD lives in src/lib/content.ts so structured data never drifts from visible text.
- Solution visuals are coded UI mockups (src/components/mockups.tsx), not images, so they stay crisp and editable.
- The opening uses a dedicated falling-card scene with lazy-loaded Matter.js physics; keep simulation and pointer listeners scoped to the scene so cards never obstruct navigation or calls to action.
- The card scene spans the opening from page top to the landing area; signal the copy reveal when cards have cleared the central content, with immediate readable content for reduced motion.
- Calculate card-pile clearance before the visible drop, use fixed-step physics with cached card dimensions, and pause settled simulations; this avoids late layout jumps and idle animation work.
- Keep opening drafting decoration in static, pointer-transparent CSS layers behind the copy and physics scene so the background adds no animation work or interaction interference.
- Keep the headache band free of grid decoration so the opening drafting grid gives way to a calm tinted surface before the dark gallery.
- Keep the headache headline full-width above both comparison columns, with matching problem/fix label rows so their graphics align.
- Keep the product gallery in its own component as a manually browsed, keyboard-accessible showcase with persistent example states; use shared content definitions so capabilities remain consistent with the site copy and never auto-advance while visitors interact.
- The homepage Hollis demo uses one browser-only reducer and shared sample records in src/lib/hollis-demo.ts; screens in src/components/hollis-demo.tsx read the same records so quote acceptance, orders, dispatch and payments stay consistent without real transactions or persistence.
- Render pricing on the homepage and pricing page through the same plan component and shared content data to prevent price and inclusion drift.
- Pub & food websites (/pubs-restaurants) have one hands-off plan: £79/month, no VAT (not VAT registered), no setup fee, price fixed 12 months; includes support and maintenance, unlimited small updates (fair use), local SEO, one standard domain and email forwarding (premium domains/mailboxes at cost). 3-month minimum then 30 days' notice; Direct Debit, site paused at 14 days overdue. The site is licensed until 12 monthly payments, then the customer's; leaving earlier it comes down unless they pay the months left to 12. No seasonal pause. Every number lives in foodTerms in src/lib/content.ts (shared with foodPlans, foodFaqs, foodAgreement, JSON-LD, llms.txt and src/test/food-pricing.test.ts).
- /terms renders foodAgreement from src/lib/content.ts, so the written plan terms never drift from the pub page and FAQs.
- On /pubs-restaurants, /terms and /chat the header shows page links (Example site, Pricing, FAQ) and a "Book a chat" that goes to /chat, so pub visitors never land on B2B pricing or the B2B contact form. The pub hero is a centered, text-only lockup on a plain paper background with the drafting grid (no photo, colour shape, or device mockup). The interactive PubDemo further down stays the accessible example. Keep owner-facing hero copy free of jargon such as "SEO" or "blog".
- The /pubs-restaurants example pub (The Hare & Lantern) is a coded mockup: one browser-only reducer and sample records in src/lib/pub-demo.ts drive the desktop frame and the phone frame, so menu, events, hours and bookings stay consistent without real transactions.
- Pub "Book a chat" links go to /chat (copy in foodChat). B2B contact stays at /contact. Old /contact?topic=pub-food redirects to /chat.
- No two neighbouring homepage sections share a background: alternate the paper opening, an ultramarine-tinted band derived from the brand signal colour, and the dark product gallery, so the page keeps visible rhythm without changing the chosen palette.
- Canonical host is https://no-default.com. Public email is matt@no-default.com. SITE.url, SITE.email, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt and JSON-LD must stay in sync via src/lib/content.ts and src/lib/seo.ts.
- Hollis Interiors and The Hare & Lantern are coded demos, never presented as clients or case studies.
